DISCOVER TADKA TALES

More Than Just Food.

Tadka Tales was created with one simple idea — to make India's incredible food culture easier to discover.

Instead of being a restaurant ourselves, we connect you with restaurants that bring these flavours to life.

India is not one flavour, one recipe or one tradition. Every region has its own food, stories and memories.

Tadka Tales brings those regional experiences together in one place.

We partner with restaurants that know their food, their region and their craft.

Their kitchens create the food. Tadka Tales helps you discover it, order it and experience it.

Discover a dish through Tadka Tales. We connect you with the right partner restaurant.

The restaurant prepares your order and our delivery process brings the experience to your door.

Tadka Tales is about discovering food, not simply ordering it. We want every dish to introduce you to a region, a tradition and a story.

DISCOVER Find dishes from across India.
EXPLORE Learn the stories behind them.

EXPERIENCE Enjoy authentic flavours at your table.

We imagine a future where discovering Indian food is as exciting as travelling through India itself.

Tadka Tales aims to bring together restaurants, regional cuisines and curious food lovers on one platform — making India's culinary diversity easier to discover, connect with and experience.
